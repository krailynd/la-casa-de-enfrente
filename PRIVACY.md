# Privacy — The House Opposite

The House Opposite keeps everything on your phone.

- **No internet.** The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- **No accounts, ads or analytics.**
- **Location (optional).** If you allow approximate location, the app reads your last known coarse position once to calculate the sunset time. It is stored only on the phone. Without it the sunset is estimated from your time zone.
- **Notifications (optional).** A local reminder at sunset, scheduled on the phone.
- **Your data.** The season, your presents list and the shelf live in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/bigdevwhale/opposite/issues.

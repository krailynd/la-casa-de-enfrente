## [1.0.0]

The first version.

- A five-storey house across the yard with 24 windows; one lights each evening after the real sunset, once the day's small real deed is done.
- 24 rooms drawn in code with their residents, December stories, things to tap, hidden clues and mandarins.
- The Secret Grandfather mystery: ten possible culprits with six habits each, clues that sharpen slowly towards the 21st–23rd, a notebook with files to strike out, an ending for each culprit.
- A detailed tree that gets a glass ornament for every lit window; "granny's" ornaments for found mandarins; the shelf keeps past seasons.
- Candles instead of streaks: relight a missed window, earn one on Sundays.
- Optional real presents behind the windows, for yourself or sealed for someone else.
- A season from December 1 or from tonight; an optional sunset reminder; Russian and English; no internet permission.
